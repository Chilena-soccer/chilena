Chilena Help & Support

Need help or found a bug? In the app, tap Settings › Send feedback. It fills in your app version and phone model for us. Or email chilena.soccer.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Your program

How is my weekly program built?

From your position, the skills you picked, your training days and session length, and your equipment. Each week’s intensity depends on how much of the previous week you completed: 80% or more steps it up, and every 4th week is a lighter recovery week.

How do I change my position, skills or match days?

Open the Program tab and tap the sliders button at the top right. Saving rebuilds this week at the same intensity, and you keep the XP you’ve already earned.

Why can’t I tap Complete straight away?

Each drill, warm-up, cool-down and workout has a timer. Start it and train: Complete unlocks after about 60% of the planned time (at least 2 minutes). The timer keeps running if you lock your phone. One timer runs at a time.

Streaks and XP

How does the streak work?

Do 2 activities in a day and that day counts. Drills count, and one timed cool-down or recovery workout can count as one of the two. After 3 days in a row you earn a daily bonus that grows each day (up to +50 XP), plus rewards at 7, 14, 30, 50, 100 and 365 days.

I missed a day. Can I save my streak?

The day after a missed day, the streak card on Home offers a streak save, once a week, if your streak was 3 days or more.

Why don’t activities I typed into the Fitness log count?

Entries you add by hand are for your records. To count towards your streak, do a workout with its timer.

Your data

I’m getting a new phone. How do I keep my progress?
  1. On your old phone: Settings › Back up to a file, and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (Google Drive, iCloud Drive or email it to yourself).
  2. On your new phone: install the app, then Settings › Restore from a file and pick that file.

This works between Android and iPhone. If you set up the new phone from a backup of the old one (same kind of phone), the app’s data usually comes across automatically.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete all my data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Reminders and the app

I’m not getting reminders.

Turn them on in the app’s Settings, then check your phone’s notification settings for Chilena. On Android, also check that battery saving isn’t blocking the app.

Why can’t I see heading drills or some gym lifts?

For safety, heading drills aren’t included for players 10 and under (and are limited for ages 11 to 13), and free-weight exercises aren’t included for players under 13. This is based on the birth year you entered.

Training safely

Chilena gives general training ideas, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new program, warm up first, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or get a headache. Players under 18 should train with a parent’s or coach’s OK, and have an adult around for lifts and jumps.
