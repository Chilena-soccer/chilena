Chilena Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Published by Lawrence Rayan

In short: There are no accounts and no analytics. Your training data stays on your phone, and we (the developer) never see it. You can back it up to a file that you control, and delete it any time. The app shows no ads.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is saved only on your phone, inside the app’s private storage:

  • Your training profile: playing position, the skills you want to work on, training days, session length, level, the equipment you have, match days, and (only if you enter it) your height.
  • Your training history: completed drills and workouts, XP, streak, weekly programs, weight-lifting and fitness logs, and progress test results.
  • Your birth year, asked once when you first open the app. It’s used to keep the app right for your age (for example, no heading drills for players 10 and under, no free weights under 13, and which ads, if any, you can see).
  • Your settings, such as reminder times, and the date you accepted the safety information.
  • Rewards you’ve earned, such as streak saves, Premium previews and Premium Passes.

We can’t see or access any of this. It isn’t sent to us or to anyone else.

Backups and moving to a new phone

  • Phone backups: if you back up your phone with iCloud (iPhone) or Google (Android), the app’s data is included in that backup, under Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy. We don’t receive it.
  • Backup files: Settings › Back up to a file creates a file with your training data. You choose where it goes (for example Google Drive, iCloud Drive, or email), and it’s only shared with the places you pick. Restore from a file reads a backup you choose.

Notifications

Training, streak and backup reminders are created on your phone (local notifications). No notification data is sent to us or to any server. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings or in your phone’s settings.

Feedback emails

If you tap Send feedback, your email app opens a message to us with the app version, your phone model and its system version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use what you send only to reply and to fix problems.

Third parties

The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools, and doesn’t share data with third parties. If that ever changes (for example, when ads or a Premium subscription are added), we’ll update this page first.

Children

Chilena is designed for players aged 13 and over. Younger players can use it with a parent or guardian; the app doesn’t collect personal information from them (their data stays on the phone). Players under 18 are asked to confirm that a parent or guardian is OK with them using the app. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example in a feedback email), contact us and we’ll delete it.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Delete all my data erases your training data on this phone.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Backup files you made are yours: delete them wherever you saved them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, such as starting to collect data, we’ll tell you in the app first.
