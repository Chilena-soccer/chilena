Chilena Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Published by Lawrence Rayan

In short: There are no accounts and no analytics. Your training data stays on your phone, and we (the developer) never see it. The free version shows a few ads from Google: they are never personalised, and players under 13 never see ads at all. If you buy Premium, Apple handles the payment and we never see your payment details.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is saved only on your phone, inside the app’s private storage:

  • Your training profile: playing position, the skills you want to work on, training days, session length, level, the equipment you have, match days, and (only if you enter it) your height.
  • Your training history: completed drills and workouts, XP, streak, weekly and themed programs, weight-lifting and fitness logs, and progress test results.
  • Your birth year, asked once when you first open the app. It’s used to keep the app right for your age (for example, no heading drills for players 10 and under, no free weights under 13, and no ads under 13).
  • Your settings, such as reminder times and sounds, and the date you accepted the safety information.
  • Rewards and ad timing: streak saves, Premium previews and Premium Passes you’ve earned, and when the app last showed you a full-screen ad (so it can keep ads to a few a day).

We can’t see or access any of this. It isn’t sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else.

Advertising

The free version shows a small number of full-screen ads at natural stopping points (after you finish a session, and after every few drills from the library), and optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward, such as saving your streak or trying a Premium drill. No ads appear in your first few days, during training, or for Premium members.

  • Under 13: no ads at all. The ad service isn’t even started, and rewards are given without an ad.
  • 13 and over: only non-personalised ads. These are chosen from the app and general information such as your rough location, not from a profile of you. The app never asks for permission to track you and doesn’t use your device’s advertising identifier for personalised ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud, Google may collect information about your device, such as its IP address, device type, system version, and how you interact with ads. Your training data is never shared with Google or any advertiser. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Premium and purchases

Premium subscriptions are bought and billed through the App Store. We never receive your name, email or payment details. To check whether you have Premium, and to restore it on a new phone, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app and your purchase records from Apple, but none of your training data. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in Settings › Manage subscription in the app, or in your Apple ID settings.

Backups and moving to a new phone

  • Phone backups: if you back up your phone with iCloud (iPhone) or Google (Android), the app’s data is included in that backup, under Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy. We don’t receive it.
  • Backup files: Settings › Back up to a file creates a file with your training data. You choose where it goes (for example Google Drive, iCloud Drive, or email), and it’s only shared with the places you pick. Restore from a file reads a backup you choose.
  • Premium on a new phone: use Restore purchases in Settings. Your subscription is linked to your Apple ID, not to us.

Notifications

Training, streak and backup reminders are created on your phone (local notifications). No notification data is sent to us or to any server. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings or in your phone’s settings.

Feedback emails

If you tap Send feedback, your email app opens a message to us with the app version, your phone model and its system version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use what you send only to reply and to fix problems.

Third parties

Apart from Google AdMob and RevenueCat, described above, the app contains no analytics or tracking tools and doesn’t share data with third parties. We don’t sell your data.

Children

Chilena is designed for players aged 13 and over. Younger players can use it with a parent or guardian: they never see ads, and the app doesn’t collect personal information from them (their data stays on the phone). Players under 18 are asked to confirm that a parent or guardian is OK with them using the app, and a parent should approve any Premium purchase. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example in a feedback email), contact us and we’ll delete it.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Delete all my data erases your training data on this phone.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Backup files you made are yours: delete them wherever you saved them.
  • To ask RevenueCat to delete your purchase records, email us and we’ll pass the request on.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, such as starting to collect data, we’ll tell you in the app first.
